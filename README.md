## 👩‍💻 About Me

I am a **Software Engineering graduate from FAST-NUCES** with a strong interest in both **Artificial Intelligence and Software Quality Assurance**.

My AI work focuses on **machine learning, NLP, intelligent agents, LLM applications and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**. I have worked with semantic retrieval, embeddings, reranking, predictive systems and AI-powered applications.

Alongside AI development, I have hands-on experience with **manual testing, API testing, database validation, automation testing and defect management**. I work with tools such as **Selenium, Playwright, Postman, Jira, JUnit and TestNG** to validate applications across the software development lifecycle.

I am particularly interested in the space where **AI Engineering meets Quality Engineering** — building intelligent systems while also evaluating their reliability, retrieval quality, grounding, behaviour and overall user experience.

I also work with **n8n** for workflow automation and API-based integrations, connecting applications and services into automated end-to-end workflows.
